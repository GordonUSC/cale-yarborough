About

A producer of integrated experiences.

My lane is the live, branded, in-person moment — the kind people remember, photograph, and bring friends back to next year. That's meant launch events at SXSW, brand activations across the Friedkin portfolio, sports nights at NRG Stadium, and most recently a three-day public festival in the Hill Country that started as a friend's ranch party and grew into something people now buy wristbands for.

I work where production craft meets brand strategy meets community taste — and I believe great events are run by small teams who care more than they should.
